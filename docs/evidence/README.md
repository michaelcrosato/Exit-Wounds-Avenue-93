# Portable verification evidence

This directory contains small reports and selected compressed frames referenced by [the verification report](../TEST-REPORT.md). The test methods and acceptance limits are explained there and in the linked method documents. A `passed` field applies to the named test procedure, not to every requirement in the design brief.

- `controller-run.json` and the `controller-*.webp` frames record the **e6426f6 patched-source** accelerated, cold-start standard-mapped Gamepad API reporter playthrough: 321 matching source hashes and Jun within handoff range. It is emulation evidence, not a physical controller test.
- `controller-run-prior.json` preserves the **e6** virtual-controller completion before the solver-mask/shadow fixes; its 361.075-second, 320-file result is historical, while `controller-run.json` is the current-source record.
- `police-matrix.json`, `late-persistence*.json`, `incoming-call.json`, `dialogue-reload.json`, `impact-*.json`, `ramp-sedan.json`, and `vehicle-comparison.json` record focused browser/runtime diagnostics. Several use deliberately staged state or actor positions; they do not count as ordinary route progress.
- `sedan-extraction.json` and `sedan-*.webp` record a focused Gamepad API replay from the real Bea checkpoint: the original sedan drives to the depot, Jun boards its passenger seat, and both physically leave through the gate. It is a saved-checkpoint replay, not a second cold-title playthrough.
- `failure-collision-gaps.json` and `failure-collision-*.webp` record focused actual-Rapier blocked-door, fixture-caused rollover/exit and depot leave/return cases. The lift plate and door walls are test-only fixtures; natural traffic-caused rollover is not claimed.
- `v07-motion-occlusion.json` and `v07-*.webp` record focused wall-blocked aimed fire, moving-car/actor clearance, service vault/cover transitions and two physical impact speeds. These are fixture-based scene checks, not an ordinary playthrough or a full motion filmstrip.
- `opening-cross-browser.json`, `release-cross-browser.json`, `firefox-active-layouts.json`, `ending-layout.json`, and `subtitle-placement-*.json` record browser and UI checks. WebKit iPhone profiles approximate Safari; real iOS Safari is unavailable here.
- `responsive-active-final.json` and `webkit-active-layouts-final.json` record final-source gameplay/menu captures at phone, landscape, tablet, desktop and ultrawide sizes. `viewport-final.json` keeps the 42-shot matrix's five Chromium software-renderer screenshot timeouts and their passing sequential retests visible. `lighthouse-final.json` records the packaged shell audit and its slow-load warnings; it is not a gameplay FPS measure.
- `run-reset.json` checks actual-game clock and stance resets across New Game, Retry and Continue. `modal-pointer-lock.json` checks that opening a menu releases mouse capture without replacing the requested screen with Pause.
- `motion-filmstrips.json` and `motion-*-filmstrip.webp` contain inspected one-frame-per-second contact sheets from the uninterrupted ordinary keyboard WebM around the service vault, van boarding and garage drive-through. The full video remains outside the repository.
- `garage-sleep-reverse.json` contains the focused before/after actual-input diagnosis of a parked van that would not wake under reverse wheel force, followed by the repaired same-session reverse departure.
- `settings-remap-recovery.json` checks a real boot with malformed persisted settings and both reserved-remap feedback paths. The controller button edge in this focused diagnostic is supplied at the app's raw-button boundary; the complete controller route uses a standard-mapped Gamepad API fixture through normal polling.
- `hardware-*.json` contain named desktop GPU frame-time samples, including the e6 1080p Low active-scene and 60-second pursuit result. They predate the e642 shadow-quality adapter; current-source low-quality frame-time confirmation is separate. Software-rendered WSL runs are not the performance baseline.
- `release-fresh-clone.json` records the public `e6cd752` checkout, install/test/build/static-preview checks, source and packaged browser results, HTTP bytes, Lighthouse limits, and viewport retests. It names raw reports kept outside the repository.
- `title-cold-hardware.json` records a single new-process/new-profile Windows Chrome title measurement on the RTX 3060 Ti; it met 10 seconds in that run while preserving the earlier slower fresh-context observation as variability evidence.
- `endurance-realtime.json` records the e6/vehicle-wake-source 30-minute native-rate hardware pass. `endurance-realtime-prior.json` preserves the passing run before the parked-van wake fix; `endurance-accelerated.json` records the separate 1,801-simulation-second pass and its real-time limits. Post-e642 endurance is tracked separately.
- `audio-mix.*` and `footsteps.*` contain browser audio captures. `audio-volume-matrix.json` records all 27 master/music/dialogue setting combinations, actual gain/output measurements, reload persistence and subtitle visibility on e6 source; it does not establish speaker/headphone listening approval.
- `rapier-panic-928d93c.json` preserves the initiating pinned-SIMD trap, deterministic snapshot and two-body reduction before the e642 app-side solver-mask guard. `shadow-quality-webgl2.json` and `shadow-quality-webgpu.json` record e642 source-browser High/Low switch regressions, each 2/2 passing; packaged and long-run checks are separate.
- `world-*.webp`, `firefox-*.webp`, `webkit-*.webp`, `subtitle-webkit-landscape.png`, `webkit-missed-call-phone.png`, and the controller frames are representative visual evidence; other full-size PNGs and large raw reports remain in the external paths listed by the corresponding method documents.

The authoritative claim status is [TEST-REPORT.md](../TEST-REPORT.md); retained earlier failures and findings are in [TEST-REPORT-HISTORY.md](../TEST-REPORT-HISTORY.md).
